## What to build / Requirements

WHAT TO BUILD

Entrants must create a working software application that uses the RevenueCat SDK to power at least one in-app or web purchase (each a "Project"). Apps must be built for iOS, iPadOS, macOS, or Android. The first public version of your app must be released between August 1 and September 30, 2026.

Students competing for the Next Gen Award follow a different, lower-friction path: submit a video and source code instead of a published store listing. No paid Apple or Google developer account required. Full Next Gen rules will be published on this page.

What to submit on Devpost

Every submission must include:

- A text description of your app's features and functionality.

- A demo video of your project. The video:

- Should show the app running on the device it was built for.

- Must be no longer than 2 minutes of essential footage — judges are not required to watch beyond two minutes.

- Must be uploaded to and publicly visible on YouTube or Vimeo, with the link provided on this form.

- Must not include third-party trademarks or copyrighted music/material unless you have permission to use it.

- A URL to your fully published app on the App Store or Google Play or Samsung Galaxy Store.

- A 1024×1024 app icon.

- At least one screenshot of the app at 1179px × 2556px with no device frame.

- Either a free trial in your app, or a promo code so judges can unlock the in-app purchase and test all premium features.

## Judging criteria

- 
 Grand Prize

 Honors the app that gains the most user traction and growth momentum during the event. Judges want to hear about what you've done post-release to push your app growth to the next level. See official rules for full judging criteria.
 

 - 
 Best Game Award

 Open category for the best mobile game shipped during the event. We're looking for great gameplay, art direction, and a monetization fit that suits the genre. See official rules for full judging criteria.
 

 - 
 RevenueCat Peace Prize

 Awarded to the project that provides the greatest social good. We're looking for apps with big benefits to communities or society at large. 
 

 - 
 RevenueCat Design Award

 For the app that best represents the craft of app development, separate from viability as a business. We're looking for innovative ideas and/or beautiful app design and animations. 
 

 - 
 Catvertising Award

 Celebrates the most creative and effective use of ads as a monetization method. We're looking for clever placements, smart integration with the rest of the revenue stack, and an experience users don't hate. 
 

 - 
 Next Gen Award

 A student-only category for the best app submitted by active students with a .edu (or equivalent) email address. Judged on a video submission and open-source code — no App Store or Google Play release required. 
 

 - 
 #BuildInPublic Award

 For developers who shared the most interesting development journey on social media. We're looking for the most compelling lessons learned, or ideas incorporated from feedback from the community. 
 

 - 
 HAMM Award

 Recognizes the app that makes the smartest use of RevenueCat to drive real revenue. 
 

 - 
 Conflict of Interest Award

 Awarded to the best overall app submission from employees of RevenueCat or any Shipaton sponsors.

 

 - 
 Influencer Award — Productivity: Christopher Lawley

 Build a polished app that helps Apple power users quickly save, organize, and retrieve reusable text, files, documents, and images.
 

 - 
 Influencer Award — Nutrition & Healthy Eating: Abbey’s Kitchen

 Create a flexible nutrition app that helps people make meals more satisfying without calorie counting, macro tracking, or restrictive meal plans.
 

 - 
 Influencer Award — Yoga & Fitness: Simone Sharice

 Create a personalized wellness companion that turns movement, Pilates, and recovery needs into a clear and achievable plan for today.
 

 - 
 Influencer Award — Gaming: Mr Lewis Blogs Gaming

 Build a gaming bucket list where players can easily save, organize, complete, rate, and share the games they want to play.
 

 - 
 Influencer Award — Career Coaching: Leadership Heather

 Help new managers practise difficult conversations, receive useful feedback, and build confidence before handling the real situation.
 

 - 
 Replit’s Idea to Income

 Use Replit to turn a new idea into a live, monetizing mobile app with growing revenue, returning customers, strong design, and real community interest.
 

 - 
 Best App for Galaxy — Samsung

 Publish a high-quality app on the Galaxy Store and make it shine on Samsung devices through thoughtful optimization, features, and foldable support.
 

 - 
 Most Viral App — Noise

 Use Noise to create engaging, repeatable content that captures attention, communicates your app’s value, and converts viewers into users.
 

 - 
 Ship Kotlin Everywhere — JetBrains

 Build a polished app for iOS and Android with Kotlin Multiplatform or Compose Multiplatform, delivering a consistent, high-quality experience on both platforms.
 

 - 
 Keep Them Coming Back Award — OneSignal

 Use OneSignal to create thoughtful notifications, campaigns, or Journeys that improve the experience and give users a meaningful reason to return.
 

 - 
 The Growth Loop Award — Layers

 Use Layers to test a focused growth idea, measure the response, explain what you learned, and show how those insights will shape your next experiment.
 

 - 
 Funnel Vision Award — Stripe

 Launch a polished web payment funnel with RevenueCat Funnels and Stripe that works reliably, converts users, and processes real payment volume.
